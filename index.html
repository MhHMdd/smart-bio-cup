About the Project

Smart Bio Cup is an intelligent beverage analyzer designed to help people understand what they drink.

4 Sensors

pH, TDS, Refractometer, and Temperature sensors work together to analyze any beverage in real time.

Health Score

Calculates a 0-100 health score and identifies the beverage type with risk level assessment.

Live Dashboard

Real-time display on TFT screen and Chrome Web Serial dashboard via USB connection.

How It Works

1

Pour any beverage into the cup

2

Sensors detect pH, TDS, sugar (Brix), and temperature

3

Arduino Mega processes data and identifies the drink

4

Health score and results shown on TFT screen + browser dashboard

The Idea

63% of adults consume sugary drinks daily without knowing the real sugar content. 64% of beverages labeled "low sugar" actually exceed the daily recommended limit. People are drinking blind.

We asked: What if your cup could tell you the truth before you drink?

Smart Bio Cup integrates multiple sensors directly into a drinking vessel, providing instant analysis without lab equipment. It empowers diabetics, patients, and health-conscious individuals to make informed decisions about every beverage they consume.

About Us

We are two young Saudi inventors passionate about using technology to solve real health problems. Our project started from a simple observation: people don't know what's really in their drinks. We combined our skills in electronics, programming, and research to build Smart Bio Cup — a device that gives instant, accurate beverage analysis to anyone who needs it.
